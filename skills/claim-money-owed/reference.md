## EU261 (Regulation (EC) No 261/2004)
| Rule | Where |
|---|---|
| 250 EUR <= 1,500 km; 400 EUR intra-EU > 1,500 km and other flights 1,500-3,500 km; 600 EUR otherwise | art. 7(1) |
| Distance by the great-circle route, from first departure to final destination | art. 7(4); CJEU C-559/16 Bossen |
| 50% less when rerouted and arriving <= 2 / 3 / 4 h late (by band) | art. 7(2) |
| Delay of 3 h or more at arrival gives the same right as a cancellation | CJEU C-402/07 and C-432/07 Sturgeon |
| > 3,500 km, 3-4 h late: 50% reduction allowed | Sturgeon, para 63 |
| Arrival time = doors open | CJEU C-452/13 Germanwings |
| Connections on one booking count to the final destination | CJEU C-11/11 Folkerts |
| Cancellation: no compensation with 14+ days notice; 7-13 days if rerouted <= 2 h earlier and < 4 h later; < 7 days if <= 1 h earlier and < 2 h later | art. 5(1)(c) |
| No compensation for extraordinary circumstances that could not be avoided with all reasonable measures | art. 5(3) |
| Technical defects are not extraordinary | CJEU C-549/07 Wallentin-Hermann |
| Strike of the airline's own staff is not extraordinary | CJEU C-28/20 (SAS); C-195/17 Krüsemann |
| Bird strike is extraordinary | CJEU C-315/15 Pešková |
| Scope: departures from EU/EEA/CH; arrivals there only on an EU/EEA/CH airline | art. 3(1) |

Official summary: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm

## Polish deadlines (ustawa Prawo lotnicze, t.j. Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1431)
- **Limitation: 1 year** from the day the flight was or should have been operated - art. 205c ust. 7. Suspended from
  filing the complaint (reklamacja) until the answer or the end of the answer period - art. 205c ust. 8.
- The airline answers the reklamacja **within 30 days** - art. 205c ust. 2.
- Complaint to the **Rzecznik Praw Pasażerów** (ULC): not earlier than 30 days after the reklamacja (art. 205a ust. 8)
  and not later than 1 year after the flight (art. 205a ust. 15).
- Before art. 205c, the Supreme Court applied the same 1-year term via art. 778 k.c.: uchwała SN 17 Mar 2017,
  III CZP 111/16.
- Sources: arslege.pl texts of art. 205a and 205c (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1431); SO Warszawa XXVII Ca 802/23 (2024).
  Re-check on isap.sejm.gov.pl before relying on a case close to the limit.

## Consumer letters (ustawa o prawach konsumenta, contracts from 1 Jan 2023)
- Withdrawal from an online/off-premises contract: 14 days, no reason (art. 27); refund within 14 days (art. 32).
- Non-conformity: seller liable for 2 years from delivery (art. 43c); repair or replacement (art. 43d), then price
  cut or withdrawal (art. 43e). The trader answers a complaint within 14 days or it is accepted (art. 7a).
- Price guarantee: only what the shop's own terms promise.
- Older purchases (before 2023) fall under the old rękojmia rules of the Civil Code.

## Tax (03-RULES-TAX-RISKS.md)
EU261 compensation is generally treated as compensation, not income; refunds are not income. Confirm in doubt.
